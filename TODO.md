## Sentence patterns (句型) that need their own practice sections

Short fixed patterns whose meaning comes from the combination, not from the individual words.

- [ ] **那就…** (nà jiù): "then / in that case…". Picks up from what the other person said and gives a conclusion or decision. Example: 那就明天见吧。
- [ ] **有什么… / 不会有什么…吧** (yǒu shénme / bú huì yǒu shénme… ba): 什么 used to mean "anything" or "something" rather than "what". Example: 不会有什么问题吧? ("There won't be any problem, right?")
- [ ] **不会有什么的。** (bú huì yǒu shénme de): a set phrase for reassuring someone: "It'll be fine / nothing's going to happen." The sentence-final 的 adds confident certainty. Its meaning comes from how it's used, not from its words. Pairs with the previous item: someone asks 不会有什么吧?, and the answer is 不会有什么的。
